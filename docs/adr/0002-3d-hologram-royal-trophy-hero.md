# 2. 3D Hologram Royal Trophy Hero Showcase & Visual Hierarchy Upgrade

Date: 2026-08-18

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The initial Hero Banner used a standard 2-column layout with a 2D line icon inside a dark flat container on the right side. Feedback during the `/grill-with-docs` session identified that the banner felt flat, lacked visual depth, and did not evoke the prestige and excitement ("WOW factor") befitting the KMUTNB Royal Innovation Awards 2026.

## Decision

We will upgrade the Hero Banner to a **3D Royal Trophy Hologram Showcase**:
1. **Hyper-Realistic 3D Trophy Centerpiece:** Replace the flat 2D icon with a custom 3D rendered Golden Royal Innovation Trophy on an illuminated cyber pedestal with volumetric lighting, gold sparks, and blue laser light rings (`/royal-trophy-3d.jpg`).
2. **Anti-Gravity Floating Glass Badges:** Add interactive floating glass chips (`👑 Grand Prize`, `💰 300,000+ บาท`) with gentle anti-gravity vertical float animations.
3. **Cyber Sapphire & Royal Gold Dual Accent Typography:**
   - `KMUTNB` in crisp metallic white.
   - `INNOVATION` in glowing solid Electric Cyan (`#38BDF8`).
   - `AWARDS 2026` in warm Royal Gold (`#FBBF24`).
4. **Streamlined Countdown HUD:** Transform the heavy black countdown box into a sleek, high-tech glassmorphic HUD bar with a live pulsing `🟢 ACTIVE` status tag.
5. **CTA Shimmer Sweep:** Implement an animated light sweep reflection on the primary submission button to draw natural focus.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Immediately establishes a prestigious, state-of-the-art atmosphere when landing on the website; clearly communicates royal prestige and competitive incentives.
- **Positive:** Eliminates visual clutter while maximizing contrast, legibility, and modern aesthetic quality.
